Our Values

Eight values guide how we advise students, represent institutions and work with families.

The foundation of our values

God Fearing

We believe that this is the source of our entire values and system.

  • Integrity

    We believe international education must be built on honesty, transparency, and responsible representation.

  • Excellence

    We encourage students and institutions to pursue high academic and professional standards.

  • Compassion

    Healthcare and education require people who understand the importance of serving others.

  • Responsibility

    We encourage students to understand their responsibility to their families, communities, professions, and nations.

  • Innovation

    We support education that prepares students for a rapidly changing technological and global environment.

  • Diversity

    We respect students from different countries, cultures, backgrounds, and educational systems.

  • Service

    We believe knowledge becomes most valuable when it is used to serve humanity.
