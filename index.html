About AIMS

Who We Are

Antony International Medical Studies (AIMS) is an international education and student recruitment consultancy dedicated to connecting students from different countries with recognized universities and educational institutions offering Medicine, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences, Engineering, Information Technology, Business, Hospitality, and other professional programs.

Our purpose goes beyond student recruitment. We aim to help young people access quality higher education, develop professional competence, strengthen ethical and social values, and become responsible contributors to the future development of their nations.

Our Vision

To build a globally connected generation of educated, skilled, ethical, and socially responsible professionals who create lasting value for their communities and nations.

Our Student-Centered Approach

How We Support You

We aim to support students throughout their international education journey, from your first counseling session to graduation and beyond.

See the Full Journey

Steps 1–4 Before Admission

  1. 01

    Student Counseling

    Understanding the student's academic background, interests, career goals, and preferred destination.

  2. 02

    Course & University Guidance

    Helping students identify programs that match their qualifications and career objectives.

  3. 03

    Application Assistance

    Supporting students with documentation and university application procedures.

  4. 04

    Admission Support

    Coordinating with partner institutions during the admission process.

Steps 5–7 Throughout Your Studies

  1. 05

    The Face Between Parents, Students & the Institution

    We stand between the parents and the institution, and between the students and the institution, facilitating day-to-day affairs and concerns related to their studies, each semester and annually.

  2. 06

    Student Support

    Maintaining communication with students and relevant institutional partners during their educational journey.

  3. 07

    Career & Future Development

    Encouraging students to develop professional, leadership, and service-oriented capabilities.
