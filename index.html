Connecting global students with quality education and building a value-based future.

Antony International Medical Studies connects students from different countries with recognized universities for Medicine, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences, Engineering, IT, Business and Hospitality, and stays between you, your parents and your institution throughout your entire studies.

  • Transparent guidance on courses and universities that match your qualifications
  • Help with documents, applications and admission
  • A point of contact for students and parents every semester
